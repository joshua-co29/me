Core Decision Factor Algorithm: Decision Score (S) = ∑ [ Wlevel × R(t) × Mstatus ] Where Wlevel is the base severity weight, R(t) is the recency multiplier (1.0 within 30d, 0.85 within 90d, 0.70 within 180d, 0.50 >180d), and Mstatus is the case status multiplier (1.25 for Open, 0.80 for Resolved).
