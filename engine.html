Staff Training and Scoring Guide

National School Code of Conduct Decision Support Calibration Reference
Ref No: MOE STG 2026
Classification: Staff Reference
Audience: Principals, Deans, Counselors and Teachers

1. Early Warning ABC Methodology and PBIS Calibration

Sentinel Edu computes a composite triage risk score from 0 to 100 across three core indicators balanced by positive behavioural recognition. Attendance Penalty (Factor A) assigns 0.45 risk points for every percentage point below 100 percent attendance, automatically recommending Welfare Officer outreach whenever attendance falls below 85 percent. Behaviour and Severity (Factor B) sums weighted referrals across Levels 1 through 5 multiplied by recency (1.0 within 30 days, 0.85 within 90 days, 0.70 within 180 days, and 0.50 beyond 180 days) and case status (1.25 for open referrals and 0.80 for resolved referrals), adding a 5 point base indicator when one or more incidents exist.

Coursework and Academic Progress (Factor C) contributes 8 points per Grade Point Average unit below 2.50 plus 4 points per failed course up to a maximum of 20 failure points. To reinforce positive conduct, Positive Behavioural Interventions and Supports merits subtract 0.40 points per active merit up to a maximum 25 point offset. To prevent points inflation, teacher awards are capped at 50 points per student over a rolling 30 day window, and earned merit balances decay by 50 percent every 30 days so only sustained positive engagement lowers risk.

3. Interpreting Trajectories, Escalation Alerts and Support Pivots

The longitudinal chart displays five historical months alongside a two month future trajectory reflecting natural progression or simulated What If interventions. Students scoring below 25 points remain in Tier 1 Preventive Support, scores between 25 and 49 trigger Tier 2 Targeted Monitoring and 1 on 5 Mentoring, and scores of 50 or higher initiate Tier 3 Guidance and Student Support Services Division intervention.

Whenever an incident history progresses from minor Level 1 or Level 2 infractions into Level 3 through Level 5 referrals, or whenever five month risk increases by 12 points or more, the rules engine issues an Escalating Trajectory Alert for immediate restorative de escalation. Where academic GPA below 2.00 or two or more failed courses co occur with disciplinary referrals, staff are prompted to consider a Vocational and Trade Course Pivot paired with Cooperative Learning rather than punitive exclusion alone. Algorithmic scores serve strictly as advisory decision support aids under Principal, Dean, and Guidance Officer oversight.
